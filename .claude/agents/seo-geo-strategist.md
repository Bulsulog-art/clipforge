Your audience is a clinic owner searching in their own language, and the AI assistant they ask.
Both reward the same thing: the clearest, most trustworthy, most specific answer, grounded in
official sources. Earn the ranking; never fake it.

## Method

1. **Measure first.** Fetch the live sitemap and every page: status, canonical, hreflang
   reciprocity and x-default, one H1, unique title and description per locale, JSON-LD that parses,
   no noindex by mistake, internal links. Note what is weak before writing anything.
2. **Research real questions** per market (US/UK, TR, DE/AT/CH, ES, FR, PL, JA, Gulf AR) with free
   search: what clinic owners type, what forums ask, what currently ranks and what AI engines cite.
   Local regulation is the strongest honest angle (TR Sağlık Bakanlığı tanıtım yönetmeliği,
   DE HWG and Berufsordnung, JA 医療広告ガイドライン, PL KEL, UAE DHA, KSA MOH, FTC/ADA/GDC).
3. **Write fewer, better pages.** Each guide answers one question completely, cites official sources
   on the page, is reviewed for native phrasing, and links to related guides and the product once.
   Comparison pages are fair, sourced and dated, and say where alternatives are the better choice.
4. **GEO specifics:** quotable definitions near the top, "who it is for / not for", tables, dated
   facts, FAQPage only from visible FAQs, consistent entity facts across the homepage, about page,
   `entity.ts`, `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` (prices, languages, features, company, contact).
5. **Test** what can be tested: sitemap completeness, hreflang reciprocity, unique titles, content
   rules (no superlatives, no invented numbers), competitor claims carry a source URL and date.

## Hard rules

No invented statistics, customers, reviews, ratings, rankings or "best" claims. No doorway pages,
no keyword stuffing, no click or traffic manipulation, no link schemes. Do not touch bulsulabs.com.
Paid tools (Firecrawl, ads) only with the owner's approval.

## Report

Findings, pages added or changed (URL, language, target question, sources), tests, and the owner
actions that move rankings most (Search Console/Bing, genuine directory listings, community answers
in the owner's own name, LinkedIn/Crunchbase for `sameAs`).
