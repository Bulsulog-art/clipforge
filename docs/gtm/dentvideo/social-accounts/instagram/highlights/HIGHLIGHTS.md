# Highlights

Five covers at 1080×1920. Each has a cream line icon centred on the brand teal gradient, inside a faint
ring. The icon stays inside the central circle that Instagram crops, as `highlights-circle-preview.png`
shows. The covers have no text, because Instagram prints the title under the circle.

A highlight can only be made from a Story. So post each Story first (they can stay up for 24 h), then:
profile → **New** (+) → select the Stories → title → **Edit cover** → pick the cover PNG from the camera
roll.

| Order | Title (as typed) | Cover | What goes in it (real material only) |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | `Örnek videolar` | `highlight-ornek-videolar-1080x1920.png` | Reels 02, 04 and 06 shared to Stories ("Add to story"), each with a text sticker: "Örnek klinik · seslendirme sentetik". |
| 3 | `Reklam kuralları` | `highlight-reklam-kurallari-1080x1920.png` | The slides of post 03 (03-1 … 03-5) as Stories. On 03-5, add a **link sticker** to `https://dentvideo.com/tr/ad-checker?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=tr-launch&utm_content=reklam-kurallari`. |
| 4 | `Fiyat` | `highlight-fiyat-1080x1920.png` | One plain text Story (Instagram's own text tool, Classic font, teal background #0f5c5c), typed exactly: "Starter: aylık 1.490 TL + KDV. Rules for every Story here: no numbers beyond the prices in "Fiyat", no claims, no countdowns, no
"limited" stickers, no music on the sample Stories (they have their own narration).
