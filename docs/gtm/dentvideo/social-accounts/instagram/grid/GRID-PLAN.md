# The first 9 posts

Every image here comes from real material:
- the three Turkish sample videos (`web/public/dentvideo/samples/tr/`)
- frames from the DE/FR/EN/PL samples
- the app's real screens (`web/public/dentvideo/landing/tr/*.webp`)
- live screenshots of dentvideo.com/tr and the free ad checker at dentvideo.com/tr/ad-checker, taken on
  2026-11-17 with the checker's own built-in "Kampanya paylaşımı" example

Nothing is mocked up, nothing is AI-generated, and there are no invented results or customers.

`grid-preview-day0.png` shows how the profile looks after post 9 (newest at top left). The layout
alternates teal, paper and video-frame tiles so the grid reads as planned rather than repetitive.
Carousel slides are 1080×1440 (3:4, matching Instagram's current grid). Reel covers are 1080×1920, with
all text inside the central 3:4 area.

Post them **in order 01 → 09** on Day 0, about 10 minutes apart. **Pin nothing on Day 0**: post 09 already
sits top left, and pinning would break the pattern. Once later posts push it down, pin 09, then 03.

Why only three of the nine posts are sample videos: Instagram cuts recommendations for accounts that
post templated or reused material (RESEARCH.md §5b). The samples prove the output, and the other six show
the product, the check and the approval.
