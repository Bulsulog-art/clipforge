## Music

All videos have a silent audio track so you can add music in the app.

- Pick something **calm and currently trending** from the in-app library (Instagram "Trending" audio, TikTok "Commercial Music Library" if the account is a business account). Soft piano, lo-fi or warm acoustic fits. Avoid anything with vocals over the text beats.
- Keep it **low**: about 15 to 25 percent volume. The text is the message, the music is just mood.
- On YouTube Shorts, add a track from the Shorts audio library in the same mood.
- Carousels: on Instagram you can add a quiet track too, it helps reach.
