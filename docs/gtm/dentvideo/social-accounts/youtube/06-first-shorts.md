# The first six Shorts

Only real material: the sample videos in `web/public/dentvideo/samples/` (real pipeline output for fictional demo
clinics) and screen recordings of the real app and the real ad checker. No actors, no AI shots, no stock clips of
our own. The sketches in `social-kit/KIT.md` need a shoot day; these six can be made at a desk while that is
being organised, and they fill the channel before the first sketch lands.

## Rules that apply to all six

- **Never post a sample on its own.** YouTube treats template-made repetitive material as inauthentic and it hurts
  recommendation (RESEARCH §5b). Every sample appears inside a real screen recording with a hook and an ending.
- **Record, do not mock.** Every screen is the live product. If the checker flags something different from what is
  written here, change the on-screen text, never the recording.
- **Test accounts only.** The app recordings use the fictional test clinic (*Örnek Diş Kliniği* for TR, *Sample
  Dental Practice* or the demo clinic for EN). No real clinic, real patient, real contact or real handle on screen.
- **Recording setup.** iPhone screen recording (or Chrome device mode at 390 wide for the website), Do Not Disturb
  on, notifications cleared, status bar cropped. Export 1080 x 1920, no music. If music is wanted, add a track from
  the YouTube Audio Library inside YouTube's own editor.
- **Text on screen** sits inside the middle of the frame: the Shorts UI covers the bottom (title, buttons) and the
  right edge (like, comment, share).
- **Voice.** Either no voice, or the owner's own voice reading the on-screen lines. Never a synthetic voice of our own.
- **No digits, prices, counts, results, superlatives or urgency** on screen, in titles or in descriptions.
- **Altered content** per `05-upload-defaults.md` §3: Yes whenever a sample or the logo preview is heard.
- **Links** in Shorts descriptions and comments are not clickable. Write `dentvideo.com` plainly anyway, and use
  the **Related video** field and the channel's profile links to carry people on.
- **TR procedure samples:** if the visible part contains the mandatory sentence ("Her cerrahi veya girişimsel
  işlemde sonuçlar kişiden kişiye değişiklik gösterebilir..."), it stays in. Never mask or cut it out mid-sentence.
