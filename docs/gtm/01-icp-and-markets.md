## Market facts

### Türkiye

- **Clinics:** TDB's November 2025 figures list **14,458 muayenehane, 1,728 müşterek muayenehane, 7,178 özel ağız ve
  diş sağlığı polikliniği and 321 ağız ve diş sağlığı merkezi = 23,685 private institutions**
  ([TDB press statement 19.11.2025](https://tdb.org.tr/userfiles/files/Basin_Aciklamasi_Fatih_Guler_19_11_2025(1).pdf),
  [TDB speech](https://www.tdb.org.tr/userfiles/files/F_Guler_Konusma(1).pdf)).
  About **43–45k dentists** (İDO-based reporting, June 2026; sources disagree)
  ([Evrensel](https://www.evrensel.net/haber/5989255/dis-hekimliginde-istihdam-krizi-derinlesiyor) **(secondary)**).
  **105 dental faculties** (TDB) → fast-growing supply, unemployment among young dentists, and more clinics competing for
  the same patients ([Evrensel, Nov 2026](https://www.evrensel.net/haber/6008160/turk-dishekimleri-birligi-dis-hekimliginde-istihdam-sorununa-son-verin)).
- **Social media:** ~65.6–68M Instagram users (75–78% of the population) in 2026
  ([NapoleonCat](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/instagram-users-in-turkey/) **(secondary)**); weekly social-media time
  25h04m vs 18h36m worldwide ([Hürriyet Daily News on Digital 2026](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkiyes-online-media-usage-surpasses-global-average-220421)).
  A 2025 study looked at Istanbul dental clinics' social-media use and its effect on patient choice
  ([DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/5121402)).
- **Willingness to pay:** clinic social agencies charge 12–18k TL (12 posts) up to 40–65k TL/month (ZBT Media, in
  `POSITIONING.md`), ZBT's blog cites 20–60k TL packages ([ZBT Media](https://zbtmedia.com/hizmetler/klinik-sosyal-medya-ajansi)).
  $29 is roughly 1,200–1,500 TL at 2026 rates ($49 for Practice: 2,000–2,500 TL): a fraction of the cheapest agency package. Owner-dentists of
  polyclinics are used to paying in USD/EUR for implants and software.
- **Pain:** *Sağlık Hizmetlerinde Tanıtım ve Bilgilendirme Faaliyetleri Hakkında Yönetmelik* (Resmî Gazete 12 Nov 2025)
  permits only informative content and bans advertising, prices, campaigns and before/after. Dentists want to keep
  posting but are unsure where the line is. "Bilgilendirme" (information) is exactly our product.
- **Extra angle:** Türkiye is a dental-tourism hub (the sector association speaks of ~250k dental tourists a year,
  undated, [Ekonomi Gazetesi](https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/kose-yazisi/250-bin-dental-turist-geliyor-agzinda-ihrac-urunumuzle-donuyor-6307)
  **(secondary)**). Clinics serving foreign patients can order videos in German, English or Arabic, which a local agency
  rarely writes well.
- **Risks:** price sensitivity and TL inflation; Turkish dentists often ask for a free trial (we don't do it: lean on the
  live preview); cold e-mail/SMS/WhatsApp to dentists is restricted (see 02).

### Spain + Mexico (Spanish)

- **Spain:** **43,672 registered dentists** (INE data for 2025, [Redacción Médica, May 2026](https://www.redaccionmedica.com/profesionales/20260527/espana-registra-dentistas-colegiados-un-mas-que-hace-ano/272349_0.amp.html));
  **23,000+ registered clinics**, overwhelmingly private ([Universidad de Granada analysis](https://odontologia.ugr.es/sites/centros/odontologia/public/ficheros/analisis-dentistas-espa%C3%B1a.pdf));
  Alimarket cites 26,400 ([Alimarket](https://www.alimarket.es/sanidad/noticia_amp/316604/un-tercio-de-las-clinicas-odontologicas-tradicionales-del-pais-cerrara-en-los-proximos-anos) **(secondary)**),
  and says a third of traditional clinics will close as chains grow → independents need visibility.
  A Spanish study found ~24% of patients used social media at their last change of dentist, website first, Instagram
  second ([zm-online summary](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/ein-praxiswechsel-entscheidet-sich-online)).
  Agencies €300–1,500/month (`POSITIONING.md`), so $29–49 is clearly cheaper but the gap is smaller than in DE.
- **Mexico:** **76,188 dental offices ("Consultorios dentales") in DENUE 2026**, most with 0–10 staff; top states Estado de
  México 10,164, CDMX 7,467, Jalisco 4,794 ([Data México / INEGI DENUE](https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/es/profile/industry/offices-of-dentists?redirect=true)).
  Social-media management from agencies runs ~5,000–22,000 MXN/month, sometimes 35,000+
  ([Cronoshare](https://www.cronoshare.com.mx/cuanto-cuesta/agencia-marketing-digital) **(directory estimate)**);
  $29 ≈ 530 MXN ($49 ≈ 900 MXN). Clinics advertising health services need a COFEPRIS *aviso de publicidad*
  ([COFEPRIS manual](https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/795383/Manual_Aviso_de_Publicidad.pdf)), so
  "checked, informative, you approve" resonates. Dental tourism (Tijuana, Cancún, CDMX) adds an English-video angle.
- **Why together:** one language, our ES site and guides already exist, and Mexico's outreach rules let us test messages
  fast (see 02). LatAm beyond Mexico (Colombia, Chile, Argentina, Peru) is reachable with the same copy later.
- **Risks:** Spanish clinics are price-aware; Mexican buyers may expect MXN invoices (Dodo receipt is a foreign invoice:
  say so up front); no MX-specific rule notes in the pipeline yet.

### DACH

- **Practices:** 102,584 dentists in Germany (2023), **30,531 single practices + 1,483 MVZ (2023)** plus group practices
  (BZÄK Statistisches Jahrbuch via [zm/ZWP](https://www.zm-online.de/artikel/2025/zm-2025-22/es-kommen-wieder-mehr-junge-fachkraefte-in-die-zahnarztpraxen)
  and [ZWP e-paper](https://epaper.zwp-online.info/epaper/11424/export-article/28) **(secondary)**). Austria and
  Switzerland add more German-speaking practices with the same content (no current counts found).
- **Willingness to pay:** German dental social agencies from €349/month with 3-month minimums, full service €500–2,500
  (`POSITIONING.md`). $29–49 is an easy budget line; practices also routinely pay for software subscriptions.
- **Pain:** § 11 HWG and the BGH (I ZR 170/24, 2025) / OLG Frankfurt before-and-after rulings; Abmahnungen are real
  money. A 2025 ZWP article on social media in dental practices is all about "Chancen, Grenzen" (limits)
  ([ZWP 07.08.2025](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/social-media-in-der-zap-chancen-grenzen-und-praktische-tipps)).
  Patients still choose mostly by recommendation (54%) and Google (13%) in a 2025 representative survey
  ([zm 2026](https://www.zm-online.de/artikel/2026/zm-2026-03/digitale-angebote-ersetzen-nicht-den-menschlichen-eindruck-vor-ort)):
  sell "trust and information", not "Instagram growth".
- **Risks:** strictest outreach law (no cold email, no cold calls without presumed consent, careful with DMs); Germans
  expect an Impressum, AVV/DPA, and German-language support (all exist on the site).

## Ideal customer profile (all three markets)

**Buyer:** owner-dentist (or a managing partner) of an independent private practice/polyclinic with 1–8 chairs and at
least one elective, high-value service (implants, aligners/orthodontics, aesthetic dentistry, paediatric). In Türkiye
the *poliklinik* owner is the sweet spot (company, several dentists, foreign patients). In Germany the
*Praxisinhaber/in* of an Einzel- or Gemeinschaftspraxis with a dormant Instagram. In Mexico the *consultorio* owner in a
metro area with an active but irregular Instagram/Facebook page.

**Signals to look for before contacting (all visible on the clinic's public business account):**
1. Instagram business account exists, last post >3 weeks ago or fewer than 4 posts/month.
2. Posts are photos/graphics, few or no Reels.
3. Website lists implants/aligners/aesthetics.
4. Posts that look borderline (prices, "ağrısız", "schmerzfrei", before/after) → the compliance pain is real (never point
   this out as an accusation; offer the checklist instead).
5. In TR: foreign-language pages on the website (health tourism).

**Not a fit now:** chains/DSOs with in-house marketing (offer Group later), clinics that want paid-ads management,
clinics expecting auto-posting.

**User inside the clinic:** the dentist approves; the receptionist/assistant or office manager often shares. Address
the dentist, but make the demo show how little the assistant has to do.

## The first 100: arithmetic

Assumptions for a 2-person team over ~60 days (to be replaced with real numbers from `/admin` after week 2):

| Step | Rate assumption | Per 100 paying clinics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clinics contacted (personalised) | — | ~2,000 |
| Reply / accept | 15% | ~300 conversations |
| Preview viewed (the live branded preview) | 50% of conversations | ~150 previews |
| Paid | ~35% of previews + partner/inbound | ~55 from direct outreach |
| Partners, associations, press, inbound SEO | — | ~45 |

That is ~35 personalised contacts per working day, split across both founders, for two months. The 30-day plan (04)
aims at the first **30–40** paying clinics and at validating these rates; the second month scales whatever converts.
