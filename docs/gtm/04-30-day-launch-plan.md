# 4. 30-day launch plan (2 people)

**Roles**
- **A — "Sales"** (whoever writes Turkish best; also runs Spanish/Mexico outreach, using a native proofreader for the first batch): conversations, demos, onboarding calls. ~5 h/day.
- **B — "Partners & product"**: partners, associations, press, listings, DACH, support, product fixes. ~4 h/day
  outreach + whatever the product needs.

Weekdays only; weekends off except launch days. Day 1 = the first Monday you start. All links carry UTMs (02). Every
"no" goes on the do-not-contact list the same day.

## Targets for the 30 days

| Metric | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 | **30-day total** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personalised first contacts (DM, LinkedIn, MX e-mail, letters) | 120 | 200 | 220 | 220 | **~760** |
| Conversations (any real reply) | 15 | 30 | 35 | 40 | **~120** |
| Previews seen (`/admin` "Saw their preview") | 10 | 25 | 35 | 40 | **~110** |
| Live demos / calls (15 min) | 3 | 8 | 10 | 10 | **~30** |
| **Paying clinics** (`/admin` "Paid") | 2 | 7 | 10 | 11 | **30** (stretch 40) |
| Partner conversations (agencies, depots, software) | 2 | 5 | 6 | 6 | **~19 → 3 signed** |
| Association/press pitches sent | 3 | 6 | 6 | 5 | **20 → 2 accepted** |

Split target for paying clinics: **TR 18, ES/MX 8, DACH 4** in month 1 (DACH is slower by design; partners pay off in
month 2).

## Daily rhythm (both)

- 08:30–09:00 — read `/admin`: new signups, new paid, failed/flagged runs (a broken first week loses a clinic).
- 09:00–12:00 — outreach block (no e-mail, no Slack).
- 12:00–14:00 — replies + demos.
- 15:00–16:00 — onboarding/support and partner calls.
- 16:30 — log in the sheet; add every "no" to DNC.

## What to measure in `/admin`

The dashboard already has these (`web/src/lib/dentvid/admin-metrics.ts`); filter by **7/30 days** and by **locale**
(tr / es / de).

| Dashboard item | What it tells you | Healthy in month 1 | Act when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founding seats left (`foundingSeats`) | The only real urgency; quote it truthfully | falls by ~1/day | Seats < 20 → prepare the post-founding offer and copy |
| MRR / revenue (`revenue`) | Money | ≥ $1,470 MRR (30 × $49) by day 30 | Flat for 5 days → change channel mix |
| Funnel: Landing views → Tried the preview | Is the landing convincing? | ≥ 15% of visits from outreach | < 8% → test hero + preview CTA |
| Signed up → Clinic saved → Saw their preview | Onboarding friction | ≥ 70% each step | A step < 50% → watch 3 session replays, fix copy |
| Saw their preview → Clicked a plan → Opened checkout → **Paid** | Does the preview sell? | Preview → Paid ≥ 25% | < 15% → offer a 10-min call to every previewer, then fix the preview |
| First week ready / Approved a video / Shared a video | Activation (predicts churn) | ≥ 80% of payers approve in week 1, ≥ 60% share | A payer with 0 approvals by Wednesday → personal call |
| Operations: failed / flagged runs, latest errors | Delivery quality | 0 failed first weeks | Any failed first week → fix and message the clinic the same day |
| Funnel by locale (`funnelByLocale`) | Which market converts | TR highest volume, DE highest conversion | A locale with views but no previews → localisation problem |

**Not in the dashboard yet (track in the sheet or via SQL on events until the "Sources" table exists):**
conversations started, demos held, paid by `utm_source`/`utm_campaign`, partner-sourced clinics, churned clinics,
time from signup to paid.
