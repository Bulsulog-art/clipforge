# 2. Lawful outreach playbook

**Not legal advice.** This is the commonly cited reading of each rule as of 2026-11-11, with sources. Where it says
"check", ask a local lawyer before scaling a channel past a few dozen contacts a week. When in doubt we choose the
channel where the clinic comes to us (content, partners, events, ads) over the one where we interrupt them.

## The short version

| Channel | Türkiye | Spain | Mexico | Germany | Austria / Switzerland |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cold e-mail to a clinic | **No** to individual dentists (muayenehane); polyclinic *companies* only via İYS → **don't** (see below) | **No** (LSSI art. 21) | **Yes, carefully** (identify, opt-out, B2B address only) | **No** (§ 7 UWG) | AT: **no**; CH: individual (non-mass) e-mail with opt-out is generally OK, mass e-mail is not |
| Cold phone call | **No** (voice calls are "ticari elektronik ileti") | Only to the clinic's published business number with a documented legitimate interest, never to a mobile of a person on Robinson lists; **avoid at scale** | **Yes** (B2B, business hours, honour "no") | **No** (needs presumed consent; usefulness is not enough) | AT: **no**; CH: OK unless the number carries the "no advertising" asterisk |
| SMS / WhatsApp cold | **No** | **No** | Avoid (WhatsApp Business policy requires opt-in) | **No** | **No** |
| LinkedIn connection + note | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes, note must be personal and not an ad; pitch only after a reply | Yes |
| Instagram DM to a clinic's business account | Yes, ask-first, manual, low volume | Yes, ask-first | Yes | Ask-first only, very low volume; better to engage publicly first | Ask-first |
| Associations, chambers, trade press, events | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (best channel) | Yes |
| Partners (agencies, suppliers, software, labs) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (best channel) | Yes |
| Paid ads (Meta/LinkedIn/Google) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

"Ask-first" = the first message contains no offer, price or link. It asks whether they want to see something. Only a
"yes" gets the pitch.

## Türkiye

**Law:** 6563 sayılı Elektronik Ticaretin Düzenlenmesi Hakkında Kanun + Ticari İletişim ve Ticari Elektronik İletiler
Hakkında Yönetmelik + İYS (İleti Yönetim Sistemi).
- Commercial electronic messages need **prior consent**. Art. 6: *esnaf ve tacirlere* messages may be sent without
  prior consent, but the sender must register those addresses in İYS and check that the recipient has not refused
  ([Lexpera — 6563](https://www.lexpera.com.tr/mevzuat/kanunlar/elektronik-ticaretin-duzenlenmesi-hakkinda-kanun-6563),
  [Yönetmelik](https://www.lexpera.com.tr/mevzuat/yonetmelikler/ticari-iletisim-ve-ticari-elektronik-iletiler-hakkinda-yonetmelik),
  [Cottgroup on İYS](https://cottgroup.com/tr/mevzuat/item/ileti-yonetim-sistemi-ve-yeni-uygulamalar)).
- A dentist in a *muayenehane* is a **serbest meslek erbabı**, not *tacir/esnaf*, so the B2B exception most likely does
  **not** apply to them (our reading; the statute lists only esnaf and tacir — **check**). A *poliklinik/merkez* run by
  a company is a tacir, but sending to it requires İYS registration as a sender, which a Montenegrin company without a
  Turkish tax number may not be able to do.
- İYS covers **voice calls, call centres, SMS, e-mail, fax and recorded-voice systems**
  ([birfatura summary](https://birfatura.com/ileti-yonetim-sistemi-nedir-iys-nedir/) **(secondary)**).
- Whether a social-media DM is a "ticari elektronik ileti" is not settled. We treat a first DM as a **non-commercial
  question** (no price, no link, no offer) and stop at the first "no".
- **KVKK:** dentists' names are personal data. Keep the prospect sheet minimal (clinic name, public business handle,
  city, date, status), no personal mobile numbers, delete after 12 months of no contact.

**Allowed channels we use in TR, in order of leverage:**
1. **Instagram DMs to clinic business accounts**, ask-first, manual, ≤20 new conversations per account per day
   (Meta treats unsolicited commercial DMs as spam; no automation tools, no follow/unfollow loops)
   ([Agorapulse summary of Instagram rules](https://www.agorapulse.com/blog/instagram-rules-info/) **(secondary)**).
2. **LinkedIn**: connection request with a personal note to dentists/polyclinic managers who list themselves there;
   message after acceptance.
3. **Dişhekimleri odaları (40 provincial chambers) and TDB**: offer a neutral, free online talk or bulletin item on
   "2025 Tanıtım Yönetmeliği sonrası sosyal medyada ne paylaşılabilir?" — never ask for endorsement.
4. **Partners**: dental depots/e-commerce (Dental Cepte, Edental, Dental AVM), clinic-software vendors (DentalBulut,
   Dr.DENTES …), dental agencies (white-label), implant/aligner manufacturers' clinic programmes. See 07.
5. **Events**: IDEX İstanbul **14–17 April 2027** (İstanbul Fuar Merkezi) ([IDEX](https://idex.org.tr/)), chamber
   congresses (e.g. Mersin Çukurova Dişhekimleri Günleri, Antalya chamber events listed in the TDB calendar).
6. **Content + communities**: Medikal Akademi's Diş Hekimliği group, dentist Facebook/WhatsApp groups **only** where
   rules allow posts by vendors; answer questions, disclose that you are the founder.
7. **Paid**: Meta ads to "diş hekimi" interest + clinic page admins, 100–150 TL/day test, landing `/tr`.

## Spain

**Law:** LSSI art. 21 forbids commercial e-mail/equivalent electronic messages without prior request or express
consent, **B2B included**; the only exception is an existing customer relationship (art. 21.2)
([Economist & Jurist](https://www.economistjurist.es/noticias-juridicas/se-puede-enviar-publicidad-o-correos-comerciales-a-traves-de-correo-electronico),
[AEPD resolutions](https://www.aepd.es/es/documento/reposicion-00187-2015.pdf)).
**Calls:** Ley 11/2022 General de Telecomunicaciones art. 66.1.b and AEPD **Circular 1/2023**: unsolicited commercial
calls need consent or a documented prevailing legitimate interest; calling random numbers is out; data from subscriber
directories needs express consent; for professionals/autónomos listed in public sources another legal basis "may"
apply ([AEPD press note](https://www.aepd.es/es/prensa-y-comunicacion/notas-de-prensa/la-aepd-publica-la-circular-sobre-el-derecho-de-los-usuarios),
[BOE Circular 1/2023](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/cir/2023/06/26/1/con/20230628),
[Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/es/spain/art/publicacion-de-la-circular-de-la-aepd-sobre-llamadas-comerciales-no-solicitadas)).
**Our rule for Spain:** no cold e-mail, no call campaigns. A DM or LinkedIn note is "otro medio de comunicación
electrónica equivalente" → ask-first only.

**Channels:** LinkedIn (dentists and clinic managers are active there), Instagram ask-first DMs, Colegios de Dentistas
(COEM etc.) and the Consejo General for a neutral talk on *publicidad sanitaria*, Gaceta Dental / Maxillaris / Dental
Tribune Hispanic editorial pitches, dental depots and clinic-software partners, Expodental (Madrid, IFEMA; check 2027
dates), paid Meta ads.

## Mexico

- No EU-style opt-in law for B2B commercial e-mail. Consumer protection (LFPC) and Profeco's REPEP protect *consumers*
  from marketing calls; the new **LFPDPPP (in force 21 March 2025)** governs personal data of identifiable people
  ([Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/mexicos-new-federal-data-protection-law-what-it-means-for-companies),
  [DOF text via Diputados](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf)). **Check** with Mexican counsel
  before volume.
- **Our rule for Mexico:** one-to-one e-mails to a clinic's published business address (website "contacto"), clear
  sender identity, link to the privacy notice, one-line opt-out honoured immediately, max 1 follow-up. Calls to the
  clinic's published landline in business hours are fine; never call personal mobiles. WhatsApp Business only after the
  clinic writes first (WhatsApp's own policy requires opt-in).
- **Clinic-side rule to mention:** health-service advertising needs a COFEPRIS *aviso de publicidad* (modalidad
  COFEPRIS-02-002-A). We never say our videos replace it.

## Germany (and Austria, Switzerland)

- **E-mail:** § 7 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UWG: advertising by e-mail without prior express consent is an unreasonable nuisance,
  **B2B included** → **no cold e-mail**. Not to practices, not to agencies.
- **Phone:** to a business only with *mutmaßliche Einwilligung* judged before the call; the mere usefulness of the
  offer is not enough (BGH line of cases); DSGVO legal basis needed too
  ([IHK München](https://www.ihk-muenchen.de/ratgeber/recht/werbung-fairer-wettbewerb/marketing-per-email-telefon-brief-etc/),
  [IHK Düsseldorf](https://www.ihk.de/duesseldorf/recht-und-steuern/recht/merkblaetter-von-a-bis-z/werbung-per-telefon-fax-und-e-mail-5852168),
  [Dr. Datenschutz](https://www.dr-datenschutz.de/ist-die-telefonische-kaltakquise-im-b2b-nach-dsgvo-verboten/)) → **no
  cold calls**. Returning a call or e-mail the practice started is fine.
- **LinkedIn / Instagram DMs:** not explicitly regulated, but a DM is arguably "elektronische Post" and can be an
  unreasonable nuisance under § 7 Abs. 1. → First message is personal and non-promotional (a question), never a
  template ad; pitch only after a reply. Keep volume low (≤15/day) and log every "no".
- **Letters (Briefwerbung) are allowed** unless the recipient opted out (a "Keine Werbung" sign or a Robinson listing):
  a short, personal letter with a printed QR code to the live preview is a lawful DACH test (cost ~€1.20/letter).
- **Austria:** TKG 2021 § 174: e-mail marketing needs prior consent (B2B mass e-mail too) → same as DE. **Switzerland:**
  UWG Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. o targets mass advertising without consent; one-to-one business e-mails with opt-out are
  generally tolerated; calls are OK unless the directory entry has the asterisk. **Check both.**
- **Channels that work here:** dental marketing agencies and *Praxisberater* as resellers, dental depots/Dentalhandel,
  practice-software vendors, Landeszahnärztekammern/Zahnärztliche Bezirksgruppen (Fortbildung talk on HWG + social
  media), trade press (ZWP online, zm, DZW, dentalmagazin), id infotage dental (autumn, regional) and IDS Köln (March
  2027, biennial — **check dates**), OMR Reviews, LinkedIn content, letters, paid Meta/LinkedIn ads.

## Gulf (UAE) — inbound only for now

UAE Cabinet Resolutions 56 and 57 of 2024 (in force 27 Aug 2024): telemarketing only 9:00–18:00 on weekdays, prior
approval, local registered numbers, Do Not Call Register, fines up to AED 150,000 reported
([Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2024/07/uae-tightens-telemarketing-regulations-what-you-ne),
[Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-unveils-new-telemarketing-rules-what-businesses-need-to-know)).
Calling from Montenegro is not an option. Use LinkedIn (English) and answer inbound; remind Gulf clinics that DHA/DoH
approvals and the medical director's sign-off stay theirs (`docs/MARKETS.md`).

## Operating rules (keep us clean)

1. **Do-not-contact list** (one shared sheet): every "no", "kein Interesse", "ilgilenmiyorum", "no me interesa", any
   unanswered second follow-up → added the same day, never contacted again by either founder on any channel.
2. **Max one follow-up**, 5–7 days later.
3. **Every message names a real person**, DentVideo, and Bulsu Labs / BLS AI Automation DOO (the Imprint page has the
   legal details).
4. **Data minimisation** (GDPR art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest / KVKK): only public business data; record the source
   (e.g. "Instagram @clinic"), date and status. Legitimate-interest note: one paragraph in the sheet's first tab.
5. **UTM every link** so `/admin` can attribute: `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=tr-founding`
   (`landing_view` stores utm_source/medium/campaign and the referring host).
